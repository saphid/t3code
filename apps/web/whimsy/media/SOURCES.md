presented as authentic footage. T3 captures are separately labeled fixtures or
integrated application evidence.

## Expanded atlas

The 51 historical entries each have a GIF selected by `../illustrations.json`.
Labels distinguish original recordings from programmatically drawn mechanism
diagrams. Static physical and authorship examples use animated explanatory
diagrams; they do not imply that the original object animated.

Additional authentic sources:

- Snoopy: Apple’s [watchOS 10 announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2023/06/introducing-watchos-10-a-milestone-update-for-apple-watch/) and `https://www.apple.com/newsroom/videos/new-watch-faces-snoopy/large_2x.mp4`.
- Activity: Apple’s [watchOS 4 announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2017/06/watchos-4-brings-more-intelligence-and-fitness-features-to-apple-watch/) and `https://www.apple.com/newsroom/videos/watchos-4-achievement/watchOS4_Achievement-cc-us-_Animation_848x480.mp4`.
- Dashboard: [MacRumors’ contemporary demonstration](https://www.macrumors.com/2004/06/28/dashboard-exposed/). The two linked MOVs were recovered from Internet Archive captures of those exact publisher URLs, then concatenated ripple-first, flip-second without truncation.
- CoverFlow: [512 Pixels’ history](https://512pixels.net/2023/10/the-history-of-cover-flow/) and `https://media.512pixels.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Original-CoverFlow.mov`. This is the pre-Apple application, not iTunes footage, and is labeled accordingly.

`idea-EX-01` through `idea-EX-24` are original animated schematic proposals.
They demonstrate intended visual behavior and are not captures of implemented
features. All have a visible proposed/schematic label.
