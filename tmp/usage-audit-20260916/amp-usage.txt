## How to Read Recorded Costs

Recorded cost is what Amp billed in dollars: to the Amp tier agent pool, granted credits, or paid credits. It never includes included orb hours, which are reported separately. Compare providers and models by recorded cost, never by tokens; a linked model provider such as ChatGPT records tokens at $0.

A linked ChatGPT subscription covers only OpenAI models. Ultra mode, custom agents or dial modes on non-OpenAI models, image generation, media viewing on non-OpenAI models, orb runtime, and tool fees (web search, web extract) always bill Amp.

Amp paid tiers have two separate pools. Agent usage covers model inference and tool fees and is quoted in dollars; orb usage covers orb runtime and is quoted only in included orb hours, never in dollars. Exhausting one pool does not affect the other. Included orb hours are counted in the tier's default orb size: a larger orb draws proportionally more included hours per hour of runtime and a smaller orb draws fewer, so included hours can exceed or fall short of wall-clock orb runtime. Current period net usage and remaining balance include restored usage credits. Range tables show gross recorded usage before those credits.

Covered is usage not debited from paid credits, such as Amp tier or granted-credit usage. It excludes Amp Free and does not represent savings from a linked model provider subscription.

Paid is usage debited from paid credits. Those credits can belong to you, a workspace, or an enterprise, so Paid is not necessarily your personal out-of-pocket cost.

Costs for linked-provider inference billed outside Amp are not included. This report cannot estimate API-equivalent costs or subscription savings, including from ChatGPT.
