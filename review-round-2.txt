I confirmed this with two captured harmless Node PIDs: `(^| )--t3code-dev-root=/tmp/q/apps/desktop( |$)` matched both `/tmp/q/apps/desktop` and `/tmp/q/apps/desktop copy/apps/desktop`. 2. **`\]` and `\}` are not proven on native macOS.** POSIX leaves "backslash + ordinary character" undefined in ERE, and `]` and `}` are ordinary characters outside brackets and braces. glibc treats them as literals (proven by the Linux test).
